Scouting Language by Quarterback Race

This visualization compares how white and African American quarterbacks were described in online NFL draft profiles. The data comes from Lund and Katballe-Kristensen’s analysis of 132 draft profiles for 66 quarterbacks.

Values represent the average number of coded descriptor concepts per quarterback. Higher values indicate that a descriptor category appeared more frequently in scouting language.
